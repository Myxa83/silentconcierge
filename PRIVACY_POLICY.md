# SilentConcierge Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 12 September 2026**

SilentConcierge is a Discord bot created for community management and Black Desert Online guild activities.

## 1. Information the bot may process

To provide its features, SilentConcierge may process information made available through Discord, including:

- Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, message IDs and role IDs;
- usernames, display names and server membership information when required by a feature;
- messages or command input when a feature requires the bot to respond to, moderate, translate or otherwise process that content;
- configuration and activity data necessary for guild features, events, tickets, roles and other bot functions;
- data voluntarily submitted by users through bot commands, buttons, forms or other interactions.

SilentConcierge does not request Discord account passwords or payment card information.

## 2. How information is used

Information is processed only as needed to operate, maintain and improve SilentConcierge, including to:

- execute bot commands and interactions;
- provide guild, event, role, ticket and moderation features;
- remember server-specific configuration;
- diagnose errors, maintain security and prevent abuse;
- provide features explicitly requested by users or server administrators.

## 3. Storage and retention

Some configuration and operational data may be stored in the bot's database so that features continue to work between restarts. Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the feature or operational purpose for which it was collected. Temporary data may be removed automatically when it is no longer needed.

## 4. Sharing of information

SilentConcierge does not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties for advertising.

Data may be processed by infrastructure and service providers required to operate the bot, such as Discord, hosting and database providers, subject to their own terms and privacy policies. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the security of the service and its users.

## 5. Discord

SilentConcierge operates through Discord and is subject to Discord's platform rules and developer policies. Discord independently processes information according to its own Privacy Policy.

## 6. User choices and data requests

Server administrators can remove SilentConcierge from a server at any time. Users may request information about stored data associated with them, or request deletion where applicable, by contacting the bot owner through the SilentCove community/support channels.

A deletion request may require a Discord user ID or server ID so the relevant records can be identified. Certain information may be retained where required for security, legal compliance or legitimate operational purposes.

## 7. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect stored information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Children's privacy

SilentConcierge is intended for use through Discord and is not directed to children below the minimum age permitted to use Discord in their country.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SilentConcierge's features or data practices change. The current version will be published at this location with an updated revision date.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the owner/administrator of SilentConcierge through the SilentCove Discord community.
